This page will teach the design choices behind vx, Turbo, Nx and Bazel,
and what each choice buys and costs, rather than a table of checkmarks.
Its widget will be a filterable matrix: pick what matters to you and see
which choices favour which tool, with the reasons and the cases where
another tool is the better pick. Every claim about another tool will link
to that tool's own docs. After reading it you should be able to choose a
tool for stated reasons.
